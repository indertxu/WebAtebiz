1. INTRODUCTION

This Cookie Policy explains how ATEBIZ ("Company", "we", "our") uses cookies and
similar technologies when you visit our websites, customer portals and other online
services associated with NEXTREPORTING.

This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Notice.

Unless otherwise indicated, references to "cookies" also include similar technologies
such as pixels, local storage objects, software development kits (SDKs), tags, scripts and
comparable tracking technologies capable of storing or retrieving information from a
user's device.


2. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website.

Cookies enable websites and online services to recognise devices, remember
preferences, improve security, analyse usage, measure performance and, where
appropriate, personalise content and communications.

Some cookies remain active only for the duration of your browsing session, while others
remain stored for longer periods depending upon their purpose and configuration.


3. WHY WE USE COOKIES

We use cookies only where they are necessary for legitimate technical purposes or
where you have provided the consent required under applicable law.

Cookies may be used to ensure the secure operation of our websites, maintain user
sessions, authenticate authorised users, remember preferences, improve website
functionality, understand how visitors interact with our digital services, identify technical
issues, enhance performance and support our marketing activities where permitted.
We do not use cookies to make automated decisions producing legal or similarly
significant effects.


4. TYPES OF COOKIES

The cookies used on our websites fall into several categories depending upon their
purpose.

Strictly Necessary Cookies are essential for the operation, security and functionality of
the website. These cookies cannot be disabled through the consent management
platform because the website would not function correctly without them.

Preference Cookies remember choices made by users, such as language preferences or
interface settings, in order to provide a more consistent browsing experience.

Analytics Cookies enable us to understand how visitors use our websites, allowing us to
improve performance, usability and content. These cookies are deployed only where
required consent has been obtained.

Marketing Cookies may be used to measure the effectiveness of campaigns, personalise
communications and understand interactions with our marketing activities. Such cookies
are activated only where consent has been provided.

Third-Party Cookies may be placed by carefully selected service providers supporting
functionalities such as analytics, embedded content, customer support, communications
or marketing.


5. LEGAL BASIS

Strictly Necessary Cookies are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in
providing secure and functional online services or because such cookies are technically
necessary for the provision of a service explicitly requested by the user. All other cookies
requiring consent under applicable law are deployed only after the user has granted valid
consent through our Consent Management Platform.

Users may withdraw or modify their consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness
of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.


6. CONSENT MANAGEMENT
We use a Consent Management Platform ("CMP") to enable users to manage their cookie
preferences.

Upon the user's first visit, the CMP presents clear information regarding the categories
of cookies used and enables users to accept, reject or configure nonessential cookies.

Consent records are securely maintained for evidential purposes in accordance with
applicable legal requirements.

Users may review or modify their preferences at any time through the cookie settings
available on our websites.


7. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Some functionalities available through our websites may rely upon services provided by
carefully selected third parties.

Where such services deploy cookies or similar technologies, the processing of Personal
Data remains subject to the privacy policies of the relevant third parties in addition to
this Policy.

The Company carefully evaluates third-party providers before integrating their services
and seeks to ensure that appropriate contractual and technical safeguards are
implemented where Personal Data is processed.


8. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Certain third-party providers whose technologies are deployed through our websites
may process information from countries located outside the European Economic Area.

Whenever Personal Data collected through cookies is transferred internationally, we
ensure that appropriate safeguards are implemented in accordance with Chapter V
GDPR.

Depending upon the circumstances, such safeguards may include adequacy decisions
adopted by the European Commission, Standard Contractual Clauses, supplementary
technical measures and transfer risk assessments.

Further information concerning international transfers may be obtained by contacting us
using the details provided in our Privacy Notice.
