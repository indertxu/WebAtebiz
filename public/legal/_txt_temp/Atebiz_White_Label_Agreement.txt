1. PURPOSE

The Provider has developed and owns the proprietary Software as a Service
platform marketed as NEXTREPORTING, consisting of a cloud-based financial
reporting, analytics and business intelligence solution designed to generate
dashboards and financial insights from compatible enterprise systems.

The Distributor has developed and commercialises the SaaS platform
KONSOLIDATOR, which is intended to operate as the primary financial

reporting layer built upon data made available by KONSOLIDATOR through

as the integrity of the data supplied to NEXTREPORTING.

The Parties wish to establish a long-term commercial relationship under which the
Distributor shall market and distribute NEXTREPORTING under a white-label model
as an integrated component of the KONSOLIDATOR ecosystem.

Accordingly, the Provider appoints the Distributor as an authorised White Label

Either Party intending to introduce technical changes reasonably expected to affect

Technical incidents affecting interoperability shall be classified according to their

response team comprising appropriate technical personnel from both
organisations.

Each Party shall ensure that appropriately qualified personnel remain available
throughout the management of critical incidents.

Each Party shall independently determine its own software release schedule.

interoperability.
