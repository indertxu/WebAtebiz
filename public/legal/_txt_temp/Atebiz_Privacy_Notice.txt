3. OUR ROLE WHEN PROCESSING PERSONAL DATA
Our role under Data Protection Law depends upon the context in which Personal Data is
processed.

Where we collect Personal Data for our own business purposes, including customer relationship
management, contractual administration, invoicing, marketing communications, supplier
management, recruitment, regulatory compliance or operation of our websites, we generally act
as Controller.

Where Personal Data is processed through the NEXTREPORTING platform exclusively on
behalf of one of our customers, we generally act as Processor, processing Personal Data solely
in accordance with the documented instructions of the relevant customer and the applicable
Data Processing Agreement.

Accordingly, where you access NEXTREPORTING as an authorised user of one of our
customers, that customer will normally determine the purposes and means of the Processing of
your Personal Data.

If you wish to exercise your rights in relation to Personal Data processed within
NEXTREPORTING on behalf of one of our customers, you should normally contact your
organisation directly. We will provide reasonable assistance to our customers in responding to
such requests where required under applicable Data Protection Law.

7. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING
Our Processing activities are carried out only where supported by one or more lawful bases
recognised under Article 6 GDPR.

Where Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract or to take steps at your
request prior to entering into a contract, we rely upon Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Where Processing is
necessary to comply with legal obligations applicable to our business, including accounting,
taxation, anti-money laundering, corporate governance or regulatory compliance requirements,
we rely upon Article 6.1.c) GDPR.

Where Processing is necessary for the purposes of our legitimate interests, including the
operation and security of our business, customer relationship management, product
improvement, fraud prevention, network security, defence of legal claims or business
development, we rely upon Article 6.1.f) GDPR following an assessment designed to ensure that
such interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.

Where consent is required by law, including for certain electronic marketing communications or
the use of non-essential cookies, we rely upon Article 6.1.a) GDPR.

Where consent constitutes the legal basis for Processing, it may be withdrawn at any time
without affecting the lawfulness of Processing carried out before its withdrawal.


8. LEGITIMATE INTERESTS
Where we rely upon Article 6.1.f) GDPR as the legal basis for Processing, we do so only after
carefully assessing that our legitimate interests are not overridden by the interests, fundamental
rights or freedoms of the individuals concerned.

Our legitimate interests may include operating and administering our business efficiently,
providing and improving our products and services, maintaining customer relationships, ensuring
network and information security, preventing fraud and misuse, protecting our legal rights,
managing contractual relationships, conducting internal reporting, responding to legal claims and
communicating with existing business contacts regarding products and services that may
reasonably be expected within the context of an existing commercial relationship.

Where appropriate, we conduct documented balancing assessments before relying upon
legitimate interests as the legal basis for Processing.

10. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS
As a general principle, Personal Data is processed within the European Economic Area.

However, certain service providers supporting the operation of our business may Process
Personal Data from jurisdictions located outside the European Economic Area.

Whenever Personal Data is transferred internationally, we ensure that such transfers comply
with Chapter V of the GDPR and that an essentially equivalent level of protection is maintained.
Depending upon the circumstances, international transfers may rely upon an adequacy decision
adopted by the European Commission pursuant to Article 45 GDPR; the Standard Contractual
Clauses adopted by the European Commission pursuant to Article 46 GDPR; Binding Corporate
Rules (BCR) approved by the competent supervisory authorities; approved codes of conduct or
certification mechanisms where available; or any other lawful transfer mechanism recognised
under applicable Data Protection Law.

Where the legal framework of the recipient country requires additional safeguards, we implement
supplementary contractual, organisational and technical measures designed to ensure that
transferred Personal Data continues to benefit from a level of protection essentially equivalent to
that guaranteed within the European Union. Such supplementary measures may include
encryption during transmission and storage, strict access management, contractual
transparency obligations, data minimisation, pseudonymisation where appropriate, enhanced
security monitoring and documented transfer risk assessments.

We periodically review international transfers in light of legislative developments, guidance
issued by the European Data Protection Board, decisions of the Court of Justice of the European
Union and applicable regulatory requirements.

Information regarding the safeguards applicable to specific international transfers may be
requested using the contact details provided in this Privacy Notice.

12. INFORMATION SECURITY AND ACCOUNTABILITY
We maintain a comprehensive privacy and information security governance framework designed
to ensure compliance with applicable Data Protection Law and recognised industry practices.
Our privacy programme incorporates the principles of accountability, privacy by design, privacy
by default, confidentiality, integrity, availability and continuous improvement.

Technical and organisational measures are selected through a risk-based methodology taking

Our security programme includes governance controls, access management, authentication
mechanisms, encryption, vulnerability management, secure software development, supplier risk
management, monitoring, incident response, business continuity planning and periodic reviews

We regularly review our policies, procedures and technical safeguards in response to
technological developments, emerging cybersecurity threats and changes in applicable
