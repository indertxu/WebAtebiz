1. PURPOSE

These Professional Services Terms ("Professional Services Terms") govern the
provision of consultancy, implementation, configuration, onboarding, integration,
training, advisory and other professional services supplied by ATEBIZ ("Provider") in
connection with the NEXTREPORTING platform.

These Professional Services Terms supplement the General Subscription Terms
and Conditions and apply only where Professional Services have been expressly
included within an Order Form, Statement of Work ("SOW") or other written
agreement executed by the Parties.

Unless expressly agreed otherwise, Professional Services are independent from the
Customer's Subscription and shall not modify the scope of the SaaS Services.


2. NATURE OF THE SERVICES

Professional Services are provided on a best-efforts basis and consist of
intellectual, technical and consulting services intended to assist the Customer in
implementing, configuring and making effective use of ATEBIZ.

Professional Services may include implementation planning, project management,
configuration of dashboards, integration assistance, onboarding, user enablement,
migration support, reporting design, financial reporting consultancy, workshops,
training sessions and other advisory activities agreed between the Parties.

Unless expressly agreed in writing, Professional Services do not include accounting
advice, statutory consolidation services, tax advice, legal advice, auditing services,
regulatory compliance services or management consulting.

Professional Services do not include verification of accounting records,
reconciliation of financial information or validation of the integrity of data
maintained within KONSOLIDATOR or any other customer systems unless
expressly agreed in the applicable Statement of Work.

The Customer remains solely responsible for reviewing and validating all reports,
dashboards, financial indicators, analyses and other outputs generated by
NEXTREPORTING before relying upon them for accounting, regulatory, tax, audit,
management or investment purposes.

Unless expressly agreed otherwise, the Service is not intended to replace statutory
financial reporting, legal consolidation processes, external audit procedures,
regulatory reporting obligations or professional accounting advice.

The Customer acknowledges that the Provider does not assume responsibility for
business decisions taken following recommendations or analyses produced during
the provision of Professional Services.


3. STATEMENTS OF WORK

Each Professional Services engagement shall be described in a Statement of Work
or other written document agreed by the Parties.

Each Statement of Work shall specify, where applicable, the objectives of the
engagement, the scope of the services, the project assumptions, the

deliverables and any specific acceptance criteria.

Where a conflict exists between these Professional Services Terms and an
individual Statement of Work, the Statement of Work shall prevail solely with
respect to the relevant engagement.

Any modification to an agreed Statement of Work shall be documented through a
written Change Request signed by both Parties.
