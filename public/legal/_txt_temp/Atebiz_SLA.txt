Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Version 1.0 · Julio 2026




1. PURPOSE
This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") forms an integral part of the Agreement entered into
between the Provider and the Customer for the provision of the ATEBIZ Software-as-a-Service
platform.

Its purpose is to define the operational service commitments applicable to the Service, including
availability targets, support services, incident management procedures, maintenance activities
and service restoration objectives.

This SLA establishes operational targets only. It does not constitute a guarantee that the Service
will be uninterrupted or error-free and shall not be interpreted as creating strict liability for every
service interruption.

Unless expressly agreed otherwise in an Order Form, this SLA applies to all standard
commercial subscriptions.


2. SERVICE AVAILABILITY
The Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure the continuous availability of
the Service throughout the Subscription Term.

The target Monthly Uptime Percentage shall be 99.5%, excluding Excluded Downtime as
defined in this SLA. The Platform availability (uptime) is defined as 99.5% measured over a
period of one calendar year, excluding planned maintenance.

Availability shall be measured on a calendar-month basis.

For the purposes of this SLA, the Service shall be considered available whenever Authorised
Users are able to authenticate successfully and access the core functionality of ATEBIZ through
the production environment.

Temporary degradation affecting non-essential features shall not, by itself, constitute
unavailability.

Likewise, temporary reductions in system performance that do not materially prevent ordinary
use of the Service shall not be treated as downtime. 3. EXCLUDED DOWNTIME

Downtime shall not be taken into account when calculating Monthly Uptime where the
interruption results from circumstances outside the Provider's reasonable control.

Excluded Downtime includes scheduled maintenance, emergency maintenance required to
address security risks, failures of telecommunications providers, failures of cloud infrastructure
providers, internet outages, denial-of-service attacks, force majeure events, failures originating
from third-party software, Customer infrastructure failures, Customer configuration errors,
misuse of the Service or suspension carried out in accordance with the Agreement.
Downtime resulting from beta features, trial modules or experimental functionalities shall likewise
be excluded.


4. PLANNED MAINTENANCE
The Provider may perform planned maintenance whenever reasonably necessary to preserve
the security, stability, availability or performance of the Service.

Whenever reasonably practicable, planned maintenance shall be scheduled outside normal
European business hours.

The Provider shall use reasonable efforts to provide prior notice of significant planned
maintenance likely to affect Service availability.

Routine maintenance activities producing no material interruption of the Service may be
performed without prior notice.

The Customer acknowledges that preventive maintenance forms an essential component of a
secure cloud environment.


5. EMERGENCY MAINTENANCE
Where immediate intervention is reasonably necessary to prevent security incidents, preserve
the integrity of the Service, mitigate cybersecurity threats or protect Customer Data, the Provider
may perform emergency maintenance without prior notice.

The Provider shall endeavour to minimise disruption and restore the Service as quickly as
reasonably practicable.

Emergency maintenance shall not constitute a breach of the Agreement. 6. SERVICE DESK
Technical support shall be available through the support channels designated by the Provider.

Support requests may be submitted electronically at any time.

Standard support shall be provided during the Provider's published business hours. Enterprise
Support, Premium Support or enhanced response commitments shall apply only where
expressly purchased under the applicable Order Form.

The Customer shall designate appropriate technical contacts authorised to communicate with
the Provider regarding operational matters.

The Provider may require verification of identity before disclosing information concerning the
Customer's environment.


7. INCIDENT MANAGEMENT
The Provider shall maintain an incident management process designed to ensure the prompt
recording, classification, investigation and resolution of operational incidents.

Each reported incident shall be assigned an appropriate priority according to its operational
impact and urgency.
Incident priorities may be revised during the investigation where additional information becomes
available.

The Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to restore the affected functionality within
the shortest practicable timeframe.

Response times represent the time within which the Provider will commence investigation of an
incident.

Response times do not constitute guaranteed resolution times.


8. INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION
The Provider reserves the right to reclassify incidents following technical assessment.


9. RESPONSE TARGETS
Following validation of an incident, the Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to
commence investigation within 1 Business Day.

The response target apply only where sufficient diagnostic information has been provided by the
Customer.


10. RESTORATION OBJECTIVES
The Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to restore affected Services as soon as
reasonably practicable.

Because software incidents vary significantly in complexity, no guaranteed restoration times are
provided.

Where immediate correction is not technically feasible, the Provider may implement temporary
workarounds while preparing a permanent solution.

Permanent corrections may be delivered through future software releases where appropriate.


11. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES
The Provider's obligations under this SLA are conditional upon the Customer providing
reasonable cooperation. The Customer shall promptly report incidents using the designated
support channels and shall provide all information reasonably necessary to reproduce and
investigate the reported issue.

The Customer shall maintain compatible internet connectivity, supported browsers, appropriate
internal security measures and qualified personnel responsible for interacting with the Provider.

Failure to provide reasonable cooperation may affect the Provider's ability to meet the response
targets established in this SLA.


12. BACKUPS
The Provider shall maintain backup procedures designed to protect production data against
accidental loss.

Backups shall be performed in accordance with the Provider's internal operational policies.

Backup retention periods may vary depending upon operational requirements and disaster
recovery procedures.

Backups are maintained for business continuity purposes and shall not be regarded as an
archival service for the Customer.

The Customer remains responsible for exporting and retaining any information it wishes to
preserve independently of the Service.


13. BUSINESS CONTINUITY
The Provider shall maintain documented business continuity and disaster recovery procedures
appropriate to the nature of the Service.

Such procedures shall be periodically reviewed and tested.

The objective of these procedures is to minimise operational disruption following significant
service failures.

The existence of business continuity procedures shall not constitute a contractual guarantee that
any particular recovery timeframe will always be achieved.


14. SECURITY INCIDENTS
Security incidents shall be managed in accordance with the Provider's Incident Response Plan.

Where a confirmed Personal Data Breach occurs, notifications shall be managed in accordance
with the Data Processing Agreement.

Operational security incidents not affecting Personal Data may be managed separately where
appropriate.

The Provider shall maintain procedures for containment, investigation, eradication, recovery and
post-incident review.


15. SERVICE CREDITS
Except where expressly agreed in an Enterprise Order Form, service credits shall not apply
automatically.

Where the Parties have agreed service credits, such credits shall constitute the Customer's sole
financial remedy for any failure to achieve the availability targets established under this SLA.

Service credits shall never exceed the Subscription Fees attributable to the affected month.

Service credits shall not apply where downtime constitutes Excluded Downtime under this SLA.


16. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
The Provider continuously reviews operational performance, cybersecurity risks, customer
feedback and technological developments in order to improve the quality, resilience and
efficiency of the Service.

Nothing contained in this SLA shall prevent the Provider from modifying its operational
procedures where such modifications improve the Service or respond to evolving technological,
regulatory or security requirements.


17. GOVERNING LAW
This Service Level Agreement forms an integral part of the Agreement and shall be governed by
the governing law and dispute resolution provisions contained therein.
