NEXTREPORTING
DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (DPA)

Version 1.0



1. PURPOSE

This Data Processing Agreement (the "DPA") forms an integral part of the General
Subscription Terms and Conditions entered into between the Provider and the
Customer (the "Agreement").

The purpose of this DPA is to establish the conditions under which the Provider
processes Personal Data on behalf of the Customer in connection with the
provision of the NEXTREPORTING Software as a Service platform and any related
Professional Services.

The Parties acknowledge that, in relation to the Processing of Personal Data
governed by this DPA, the Customer acts as Controller and the Provider acts as
Processor within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), unless
expressly agreed otherwise in writing with respect to a specific Processing activity.
Nothing contained in this DPA shall prevent the Provider from acting as an
independent Controller in relation to Personal Data processed for its own legitimate
business purposes, including customer relationship management, invoicing, legal
compliance, fraud prevention, network security and the management of its
contractual relationship with the Customer.


2. SCOPE OF PROCESSING

The Provider shall Process Personal Data solely for the purpose of providing the
Services described in the Agreement and strictly in accordance with the
documented instructions of the Customer.

The Parties acknowledge that the nature of the Service necessarily requires the
Provider to host, organise, store, retrieve, analyse, transmit and otherwise Process
Personal Data made available through the Service or received through authorised
integrations with third-party systems designated by the Customer.

The categories of Data Subjects, the categories of Personal Data, the nature of the
Processing operations and the purposes of the Processing are described in Annex
I, which forms an integral part of this DPA.
Should the Customer require Processing activities materially different from those
described in Annex I, the Parties shall agree upon such Processing in writing before
implementation.


3. PROCESSING INSTRUCTIONS

The Provider shall Process Personal Data exclusively on documented instructions
received from the Customer, including those reflected in the Agreement, the
applicable Order Form, this DPA and any subsequent written instructions agreed
between the Parties.

If the Provider considers that an instruction infringes applicable Data Protection
Law, it shall promptly inform the Customer and may suspend the implementation
of such instruction until the Customer confirms, modifies or withdraws it.

The Provider shall not be required to follow any instruction which, in its reasonable
opinion, would expose the Provider to a breach of applicable law, regulatory
requirements or legally binding orders issued by competent authorities.

Where the Provider is required under applicable law to Process Personal Data
otherwise than pursuant to the Customer's instructions, it shall inform the
Customer before carrying out such Processing unless prohibited by law on
important grounds of public interest.

The Customer acknowledges that general instructions regarding the operation of
the Service may be implemented through configuration settings selected directly by
the Customer or its Authorised Users.


4. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Provider shall ensure that every individual authorised to Process Personal Data
is bound by an appropriate contractual or statutory duty of confidentiality.

Access to Personal Data shall be strictly limited to those members of personnel
who require such access for the performance of their assigned responsibilities.

The Provider shall maintain internal procedures designed to ensure that
confidentiality obligations continue to apply after termination of employment or
contractual engagement.

The Provider shall regularly remind relevant personnel of their confidentiality
obligations and shall implement appropriate disciplinary measures in the event of
unauthorised disclosure.
5. SECURITY OF PROCESSING

Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, the nature,
scope, context and purposes of the Processing, together with the risks of varying
likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of natural persons, the Provider
shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures
designed to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risks presented by the
Processing.

Such measures shall be continuously reviewed throughout the duration of the
Agreement and may be updated by the Provider provided that the overall level of
protection is not materially reduced.

Without limiting the foregoing, the Provider shall maintain a comprehensive
information security programme, which may address, among others matters:
governance, access management, authentication, encryption, system monitoring,
vulnerability management, malware protection, secure software development,
backup management, business continuity, disaster recovery, incident response
and physical security.

The specific security measures implemented by the Provider are described in
Annex II, which may be updated from time to time to reflect technological
developments, recognised industry standards and evolving cybersecurity risks.

The Provider shall not materially reduce the level of protection described in Annex
II without prior notice to the Customer.


6. ASSISTANCE TO THE CUSTOMER

The Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to enable the Customer to comply
with its obligations under applicable Data Protection Law, taking into account the
nature of the Processing and the information available to the Provider.

Such assistance shall include, where reasonably necessary, support in responding
to requests from Data Subjects, cooperation in relation to supervisory authority
investigations, assistance in conducting data protection impact assessments and
support in consultations with competent supervisory authorities where required by
law.

The Provider shall not respond directly to requests received from Data Subjects
unless legally required to do so or expressly authorised by the Customer.
Where legally permissible, the Provider shall promptly notify the Customer upon
receipt of any request, complaint, enquiry or investigation relating to Personal Data
Processed under this DPA.
Where assistance requires substantial additional resources beyond the ordinary
provision of the Service, the Parties may agree reasonable additional compensation
reflecting the actual effort involved.


7. PERSONAL DATA BREACHES

The Provider shall maintain documented procedures for identifying, managing,
investigating and remediating Personal Data Breaches.

Upon becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Personal Data
Processed under this DPA, the Provider shall notify the Customer without undue
delay and, wherever reasonably practicable, within seventy-two (72) hours after
confirming that a reportable breach has occurred.

Such notification shall include all information reasonably available to the Provider
concerning the nature of the incident, the categories of Personal Data affected, the
likely consequences of the breach, the measures already implemented and any
proposed remediation measures.

Where all relevant information cannot reasonably be provided simultaneously, the
Provider may supply information in phases as it becomes available.

The Provider shall promptly investigate every confirmed Personal Data Breach and
shall implement appropriate corrective actions designed to mitigate its effects and
reduce the likelihood of recurrence.

Notification of a Personal Data Breach shall not constitute an admission of liability
or fault.


8. SUB-PROCESSORS

The Customer grants the Provider a general authorisation to engage Sub-
processors for the performance of the Services.

The Provider shall ensure that every Sub-processor is engaged under a written
agreement imposing data protection obligations that are no less protective than
those contained in this DPA insofar as applicable to the Processing entrusted to the
relevant Sub-processor.

The Provider shall remain fully responsible for the performance of its Sub-
processors' obligations under this DPA.

The current list of authorised Sub-processors shall be maintained in Annex III.
The Provider may appoint additional Sub-processors provided that it gives the
Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior notice.

If the Customer reasonably objects to the appointment of a proposed Sub-
processor on documented data protection grounds, the Parties shall cooperate in
good faith to identify a commercially reasonable solution.

If no reasonable solution can be achieved, the Customer may terminate only the
affected Services without penalty.


9. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

The Provider shall not transfer Personal Data to a country or international
organisation outside the European Economic Area unless such transfer is carried
out in accordance with Chapter V of the GDPR.

Where the Provider or any authorised Sub-processor transfers Personal Data to a
third country for which no adequacy decision has been adopted by the European
Commission, the Provider shall ensure that appropriate safeguards have been
implemented prior to such transfer.

Such safeguards may include the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the
European Commission, an approved certification mechanism, Binding Corporate
Rules or any other lawful transfer mechanism recognised under applicable Data
Protection Law.

Where required by applicable law, the Provider shall conduct and periodically
review an assessment of the legal framework applicable to the recipient jurisdiction
in order to determine whether the implemented safeguards continue to ensure a
level of protection essentially equivalent to that guaranteed within the European
Union.

Where supplementary contractual, organisational or technical measures are
reasonably necessary to ensure the lawfulness of a transfer, the Provider shall
implement such measures before the transfer takes place.

The Provider shall promptly notify the Customer if it becomes aware that an
applicable transfer mechanism can no longer be relied upon or if a competent
authority challenges the lawfulness of a transfer affecting the Services.


10. AUDITS AND COMPLIANCE

The Provider shall maintain appropriate records demonstrating compliance with
this DPA and shall make available to the Customer such information as is
reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 of the GDPR.
The Parties acknowledge that modern cloud services are operated within highly
secure, multi-tenant environments where unrestricted physical access or
customer-led inspections may compromise the security, confidentiality or
availability of services provided to other customers.

Accordingly, the Customer agrees that the Provider may satisfy its audit obligations
by making available appropriate independent audit reports, recognised
certifications, compliance attestations, penetration testing summaries or
equivalent documentation demonstrating compliance with applicable security
standards.

Where such documentation does not reasonably satisfy the Customer's regulatory
obligations, the Customer may request an additional audit.

Any such audit shall: a) be limited to matters directly relevant to the Processing
carried out under this DPA; b) be conducted during normal business hours; c) be
subject to reasonable prior written notice of not less than thirty (30) days; d) avoid
unnecessary disruption of the Provider's business operations; e) preserve the
confidentiality of information relating to other customers; and f) be carried out by
an independent professional auditor bound by appropriate confidentiality
obligations.

Unless the audit reveals a material breach of this DPA or applicable Data Protection
Law, the Customer shall bear its own costs and reimburse the Provider for the
reasonable internal costs incurred in facilitating the audit.

The Provider shall promptly remedy any material deficiencies identified during an
audit.


11. RETURN AND DELETION OF PERSONAL DATA

Upon termination or expiration of the Agreement, the Customer shall remain
responsible for exporting any Personal Data it wishes to retain using the
functionalities made available through the Service.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing or required by applicable law, the Provider shall
retain Personal Data for the limited period specified in the Agreement solely to
facilitate Customer retrieval, disaster recovery and the orderly termination of the
Services.

Following expiry of the applicable retention period, the Provider shall securely
delete or irreversibly anonymise all remaining Personal Data under its control.

The obligation to delete Personal Data shall not apply where retention is required
under applicable law or where Personal Data forms part of routine backup media
retained in accordance with the Provider's documented backup policies, provided
that such information remains subject to appropriate technical and organisational
safeguards and is not actively processed except where legally required.

Upon reasonable request, the Provider shall confirm completion of the deletion
process.


12. GOVERNMENT ACCESS REQUESTS

If the Provider receives a legally binding request from a public authority requiring
access to Personal Data Processed under this DPA, the Provider shall, to the extent
legally permitted, promptly notify the Customer before disclosing the requested
information.

The Provider shall review every such request to determine whether it is valid,
proportionate and legally enforceable.

Where the Provider reasonably considers that a request exceeds the powers
granted under applicable law or otherwise lacks a lawful basis, it shall use
commercially reasonable efforts to challenge or limit the request before disclosing
any Personal Data.

The Provider shall disclose only the minimum amount of Personal Data strictly
required to comply with the applicable legal obligation.

Nothing in this Clause shall require the Provider to act contrary to applicable law.


13. LIABILITY

Each Party shall remain responsible for its respective obligations under applicable
Data Protection Law.

Nothing contained in this DPA shall alter or expand the allocation of liability
established under the Agreement except where expressly required by mandatory
law.

The Provider shall be liable only for damages resulting directly from its own breach
of this DPA or applicable Data Protection Law.

Where multiple parties are responsible for the same damage, liability shall be
allocated between them in proportion to their respective responsibility for the event
giving rise to the damage.

The limitations of liability established in the Agreement shall apply equally to this
DPA except where mandatory law provides otherwise.
14. TERM

This DPA shall become effective on the Effective Date of the Agreement.

It shall remain in force for so long as the Provider continues to Process Personal
Data on behalf of the Customer.

Termination of the Agreement shall automatically terminate this DPA, except for
those provisions which by their nature are intended to survive termination, including
those relating to confidentiality, deletion of Personal Data, liability and applicable
law.


15. GOVERNING LAW

This Data Processing Agreement shall be governed by the governing law and
jurisdiction provisions contained in the Agreement of which it forms an integral part.

The Provider maintains an Information Security Management System designed to
protect the confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience of the Services.

maintains measures addressing identity and access management, role-based
access control, authentication mechanisms, encryption of data in transit and at
rest where appropriate, infrastructure monitoring, security logging, vulnerability

security, business continuity, disaster recovery, supplier risk management,
personnel security, incident response and periodic security assessments.

The Provider regularly reviews its security measures in light of technological
developments, emerging threats and recognised industry standards.

protection is not materially reduced.
